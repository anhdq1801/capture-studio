Refund Policy

Last updated [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]

The short version

Write to [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] within [[REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS]] days of a payment and we will refund it in full. You do not have to explain why.

Why we can offer that

Everything that makes Capture Studio useful — capturing, annotating, text recognition, recording, the optimiser — is free and needs no account. You can decide whether the app suits you before spending anything. A plan only adds cloud storage and shareable links, so the only thing you are risking money on is a feature you can test in a few minutes.

How to ask

Email [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] from the address on the account. Tell us which payment you mean; a date and an amount is enough. We answer within [[SUPPORT_RESPONSE_DAYS]] business days, and refunds go back to the original payment method. How long it then takes to appear is up to your bank, usually five to ten business days.

What happens to your uploads

A refund ends the plan. Files you have already uploaded stay reachable for [[GRACE_DAYS]] days, exactly as they would if the plan had simply expired, and are then deleted. Nothing on your own computer is affected.

When we will say no

  • More than [[REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS]] days have passed since the payment. Cancel instead — your plan then runs to the end of the period you have paid for and does not renew.
  • The account was suspended for breaking the Terms of Service.
  • The pattern shows repeated subscribe-and-refund cycles on the same account or payment method.

Cancelling instead of refunding

Cancelling stops the next charge and nothing else: you keep the plan, and your links keep working, until the period you paid for runs out. That is usually what people want when they are simply done with a project.

If you paid through a reseller

Payments are handled by [[PAYMENT_PROVIDER]], who may be the seller of record for your purchase. Where that is the case their refund terms also apply, and they may handle the refund directly. Write to us either way and we will point you to the right place.

Please talk to us before a chargeback

A chargeback costs us a fee and closes the account automatically, which usually is not what the person wanted. An email gets you the same money back faster.
