Privacy Policy

Last updated [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]

This explains what [[LEGAL_ENTITY]] stores when you use Capture Studio, why, and for how long. The short version: without an account we store nothing at all, and with one we store your email, your billing status, and the files you choose to upload.

Screenshots stay on your computer

Capture Studio is a desktop application. Screenshots, recordings and annotations are written to a folder on your own disk that you choose, and are never sent anywhere by default. There is no background sync and no telemetry about what you capture.

A capture leaves your computer only when you press Upload on that specific capture, and only if you have an account with an active plan.

What we store when you have an account

  • Your email address — to identify the account, and to contact you about the service.
  • Your password, hashed — put through a one-way function with a random salt. We cannot read it, and we cannot tell you what it is.
  • Subscription and payment records — which plan you are on, when the period ends, the amount and currency of each payment, and the reference our payment provider gave it. We never see or store your card number.
  • Files you upload, and for each one its size, the time it was uploaded, and the identifier it has inside the app. The size is what your storage quota is measured against.

We do not run advertising, we do not build profiles, and we do not sell or rent any of this to anybody.

Uploaded files are public to anyone with the link

This is the most important thing on this page. An upload gets a URL that works for anyone who has it. It is not indexed and not listed, but it is not protected either — no password, no login check. Anyone you send the link to can pass it on.

Treat every upload as public. Do not upload screenshots containing passwords, keys, personal data belonging to other people, or anything else you would not put on a public web page.

We may open an uploaded file when investigating a report of abuse or a support request you have raised. We do not otherwise look at them.

Where it is stored

Accounts, subscriptions and upload records are held in a Cloudflare D1 database. Uploaded files are held in Cloudflare R2 object storage. Both run on Cloudflare's global infrastructure, which means your data may be processed outside the country you live in.

Payments are processed by [[PAYMENT_PROVIDER]], who handle the card details themselves and pass us only the result. Their own privacy policy covers that part.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded files: for as long as your plan is active. If it ends, they are kept for [[GRACE_DAYS]] days — so links you already shared keep working through a failed card or a forgotten renewal — and then permanently deleted.
  • Interrupted uploads: a file that started uploading but never completed is deleted automatically within a few hours.
  • A file you delete: removed from storage, and the space returns to your quota.
  • Account and payment records: kept while the account exists, and after closure only as long as tax and accounting rules require us to keep proof of a transaction.

Your rights

Deleting everything is a button. In Capture Studio, open Settings → Account and choose "Delete cloud account". It removes every file you have uploaded, the links to them, and your email address, straight away. Screenshots in your local library on your own computer are not touched.

We keep the record of any payment you made — the amount, the date and the provider's reference — because tax and accounting rules require proof of a transaction. That record is not linked to your email address once the account is deleted.

You can also ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to correct it. Write to [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] from the address the account uses and we will act within 30 days.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The application signs you in with a token stored locally on your own computer, which expires after 30 days.

Children

Capture Studio is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored hashed and salted. Uploads are authorised individually by our server before they can be written. No system is perfect; if we discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page, and for material changes we will email account holders.
