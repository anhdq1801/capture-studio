Plans

The app is free. Storage is what you pay for.

Capturing, annotating, text recognition, recording and the image optimiser cost nothing and work without an account. A plan adds cloud upload and shareable links, and the only thing that changes between plans is how much you can store.

    Plans could not be loaded just now. Open Capture Studio and go to Settings → Account for current prices, or email [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] and we will send them to you.

    What a plan includes

    • Upload

      Send any capture to the cloud from the editor or the detail view, and get a link on your clipboard.

    • Links

      A direct URL to the image. Anyone with the link can open it, so treat uploads as public.

    • Storage

      Your quota covers everything you have uploaded. Delete an upload and the space comes back.

    Answers before you buy

    • Ending

      What happens if I stop paying?

      Uploaded files and their links keep working for 30 days after a plan ends, then they are deleted. Everything in your local library stays on your computer, untouched.

    • Changing

      Can I move between plans?

      Yes. If you move down to a plan smaller than what you are storing, you will need to delete some uploads before the smaller quota applies.

    • Refunds

      What if it is not for me?

      See the refund policy. Short version: write to us within [[REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS]] days of a payment and we will refund it.
